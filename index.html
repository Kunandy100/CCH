Before you sit the exam

Exam questions, answered

The things people ask most before booking a cloud certification exam.

How long should I study before taking the exam?+

Most people are ready for a foundational exam (AWS CCP, AZ-900) in 3–5 weeks of steady study — about an hour a day plus hands-on labs. The multi-cloud track takes a little longer since it covers three providers.

Is the exam taken online or at a test center?+

Both. You can sit most cloud exams from home with online proctoring, or in person at a testing center. We help you choose and walk through the check-in steps for each.

What score do I need to pass?+

It varies by provider — AWS and Azure foundational exams both pass around 700/1000. You don’t need perfection, just consistent readiness, which our practice checks confirm before you book.

Do I need IT experience before starting?+

No. The foundational tracks are built for beginners and start from the ground up. General computer comfort helps, but no prior cloud or coding background is required.

How much does the exam itself cost?+

Exam fees are paid to the provider, separate from training — foundational exams typically run about $100 USD. We’ll point you to any available discounts or voucher programs.

What if I don’t pass on the first try?+

It happens, and it’s not the end. Providers allow retakes after a short waiting period. We review what tripped you up, target those areas, and get you ready for round two.
